# GawaGo Color Palette

All-green palette — no other hue mixed in. Tints/shades of one green family.

## Colors

| Role | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#1F2A19` | Page background (deep moss green) |
| Panel / Card | `#29341F` | Stats card background (slightly lighter moss green) |
| Accent | `#9EBF5E` | CTA button, "trusted" highlight, big stat number, badges, dots |
| Text — Primary | `#F5F1E6` | Headlines, body copy, stat numbers |
| Text — Muted | `#ADB79C` | Subtext, captions |
| Text — Muted (on card) | `#C2CBAF` | Stat labels inside the card |
| Text — on Accent buttons | `#1B2412` | Dark text sitting on top of the accent green |

## Accent alternates (swap `#9EBF5E` with any of these)

- `#8AAF4C` — deeper, more saturated leaf green
- `#B0CC77` — lighter, softer green

## Background alternates (swap `#1F2A19` with any of these)

- `#232E1B`
- `#1B2416`
- `#26311D`

## Notes

- Border / divider lines use the primary text color at low opacity: `rgba(245,241,230,0.12)` (general borders), `rgba(245,241,230,0.2)` (dashed divider).
- Accent-tinted borders/backgrounds (badges, pills) use the accent color at low opacity: `rgba(158,191,94,0.4)` border, `rgba(158,191,94,0.1)` fill.
